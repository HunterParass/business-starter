# Assumption register

> Every number or claim the plan depends on that is not proven yet.
> Departments propose assumptions in their reports; HQ gives them IDs and records them here.
>
> Confidence: **H** = proven by customers or sales, **M** = sourced desk research, **L** = educated guess.
> The plan is only as strong as its riskiest L-rated assumption. Test those first.
