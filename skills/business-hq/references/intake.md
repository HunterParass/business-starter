# Intake: interviewing the founder

HQ fills `00-hq/BRIEF.md` from this interview. Keep it fast: the founder came to build, not to fill in a form.

## Before asking anything

Extract everything the founder already said (idea, location, budget, skills) and only ask for what's missing. If they gave no idea at all, ask in plain text what they want to build or what problem they keep noticing, before any multiple-choice round.

## Round 1: the shape of the business (AskUserQuestion, up to 4)

1. **Business type**: SaaS or app / game / ecommerce or physical product / service or freelance / local business / marketplace / content or media.
2. **Stage**: just an idea / validating / building / already selling.
3. **Goal for the next 12 months**: learn and earn a bit / steady side income / replace a salary / build something venture-scale.
4. **Starting budget**: under 500 / 500-2,000 / 2,000-10,000 / more (in their currency).

## Round 2: the founder and the rules they live under (up to 4)

1. **Where they're based**: country and province or state. Ask as free text if needed. Never infer it from time zone or language. It decides structure, registration, tax and licence answers.
2. **Hours per week** they can give: under 5 / 5-15 / 15-30 / full time.
3. **Age bracket**: 18 or older / under 18. Under 18 changes what they can sign, register or open (contracts, bank and payment accounts, platform payouts), so Legal needs it. Ask neutrally and don't comment further.
4. **Unfair advantages** (multi-select): a skill that's hard to hire / access to the customers / an existing audience / industry experience / a co-founder / none yet.

## Round 3: only if still missing (up to 4)

1. **Evidence so far**: none / conversations / waitlist or pre-orders / paying customers.
2. **First customer**: who exactly, in one line (free text).
3. **Time to first revenue needed**: no rush / within 3 months / within 1 month.
4. **Hard no's**: things they won't do (cold calling, paid ads, quitting school or work, taking investment).

## Defaults when a question goes unanswered

| Field | Default | Mark as |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | under 1,000 in local currency | `[DEFAULT]` |
| Hours | 10 per week | `[DEFAULT]` |
| Goal | steady side income | `[DEFAULT]` |
| Stage | idea | `[DEFAULT]` |
| Jurisdiction | no default: leave Legal out of the wave and ask again at the next gate | `[MISSING]` |
| Age bracket | 18 or older, and Legal adds a short under-18 section anyway | `[DEFAULT]` |

## Closing the intake

Read back, in five lines: the one-liner, first customer, model guess, constraints (budget, hours, place), and the mode. Fix anything the founder corrects, update the brief's changelog, then start Wave 1.
