# Ad library scan

What competitors are paying to say is some of the best free market research there is: the pains they lead with, the offers they test, and which ads they keep running. The ad platforms publish this in public libraries. They are JavaScript apps, so web search and page fetching can't read them; they need a real browser.

## Who runs it

- **HQ runs it** when the main chat has a browser tool (Claude in Chrome, the built-in browser, or a browser MCP). Department subagents don't have browser tools. HQ saves the result as `02-competitors/ad-library.md` on the Competitors department's behalf (the one exception to "departments own their folders") and logs it in PROGRESS.
- **Without a browser**, the Competitors department writes `02-competitors/ad-library.md` as a checklist: the libraries below, the exact names and domains to search for each competitor, and the table to fill. It says plainly that no ads were collected. The founder can do it in about 15 minutes.
- **Never describe ads that weren't opened.** No "competitors are probably running..." guesses.

## Where to look (pick the ones the playbook's customers use)

| Library | Covers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Ad Library: facebook.com/ads/library | Facebook, Instagram, Messenger | All active ads from any advertiser, any country. Inactive ads only for political ads and the EU/UK. Search the page name |
| Google Ads Transparency Center: adstransparency.google.com | Search, YouTube, Display | Search by advertiser name or website domain |
| LinkedIn Ad Library: linkedin.com/ad-library | LinkedIn | Best for B2B and services sold to businesses |
| TikTok Top Ads: TikTok Creative Center (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) | TikTok | Top-performing ads by industry and region; the TikTok Ad Library itself covers EU ads only |

## What to record (per ad, 5-15 ads per competitor at most)

| Competitor | Platform | Running since | Format | Hook (first line or first 3 seconds) | Pain or angle | Offer and price | Call to action | Landing page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then write, in plain words:
1. **Angles everyone uses** (the table stakes) and **angles nobody uses** (possible openings).
2. **Long runners:** ads live for 60+ days are a signal the advertiser thinks they work. A signal, not proof of profit.
3. **Offers and prices** that appear in ads, dated.
4. **What to test**: 2-3 angles or hooks for the founder's own content or ads, each tied to a pain from `03-customers/` research, using only claims the founder can prove.

## Rules

- Record what you saw with the date; screenshots are optional and stay in the founder's own files.
- Don't copy a competitor's creative, wording or look. Learn the angle, write your own.
- Ad spend and results aren't shown in these libraries (except political ads). Don't estimate them.
