# Credits and inspiration

Business Starter is original work, but its design borrows *ideas* (not text or code) from these open-source projects. Go star them.

| Project | License | Idea borrowed |
|---|---|---|
| [ferdinandobons/startup-skill](https://github.com/ferdinandobons/startup-skill) | MIT | Phased startup design with resume-from-checkpoint, research waves, a go/no-go gate before strategy, a customer discovery gate, radical honesty |
| [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT | One shared context document every skill reads first (our `00-hq/BRIEF.md`), a simulated advisory council built to disagree, and the scope of the social, short-form video, creator partnership, offer design, referral, churn and lead magnet coverage |
| [lool-ventures/founder-skills](https://github.com/lool-ventures/founder-skills) | Apache-2.0 | Deterministic scripts for maths instead of letting the model estimate, red-team verification, investment committee simulation |
| [alirezarezvani/claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills) | MIT | A virtual executive team with a chief-of-staff router and artifact handoffs between roles |
| [emotixco/claude-skills-founder](https://github.com/emotixco/claude-skills-founder) | MIT | A multi-dimension idea scorecard ending in a build / pivot / kill verdict, MVP scoping, interview scripts |
| [wshobson/agents](https://github.com/wshobson/agents) (startup-business-analyst) | MIT | Bottom-up market sizing with triangulation, startup metrics by business model, team composition analysis |
| [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) (small-business, marketing, legal) | Apache-2.0 | Approval before anything touches money or customers, working from pasted data when nothing is connected, severity-by-likelihood legal risk, reviews and reputation, inventory velocity and reorders, win-back |
| [ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills](https://github.com/ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills) (domain-name-brainstormer, competitive-ads-extractor) | Apache-2.0 | Checking domains while naming (rebuilt as `domain_check.py` with real registry lookups) and mining competitors' ads from public ad libraries (rebuilt as the browser-only ad library scan) |
| [claude-office-skills/skills](https://github.com/claude-office-skills/skills) | MIT | The starter document kit (invoice, proposal, NDA and contract outlines, expense tracking, weekly report), brand guidelines, the hook-content-CTA video script structure, hiring kit |

Frameworks referenced by name belong to their authors: Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya), *The Mom Test* (Rob Fitzpatrick), *Obviously Awesome* positioning (April Dunford), *Traction* and the Bullseye framework (Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares), Jobs to be Done (Clayton Christensen and others), the Van Westendorp price sensitivity meter, AARRR (Dave McClure), and the offer value equation (Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*).
