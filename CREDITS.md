# Credits and inspiration

Business Starter is original work, but its design borrows *ideas* (not text or code) from these open-source projects. Go star them.

| Project | License | Idea borrowed |
|---|---|---|
| [ferdinandobons/startup-skill](https://github.com/ferdinandobons/startup-skill) | MIT | Phased startup design with resume-from-checkpoint, research waves, a go/no-go gate before strategy, a customer discovery gate, radical honesty |
| [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) | MIT | One shared context document every skill reads first (our `00-hq/BRIEF.md`), a simulated advisory council built to disagree |
| [lool-ventures/founder-skills](https://github.com/lool-ventures/founder-skills) | Apache-2.0 | Deterministic scripts for maths instead of letting the model estimate, red-team verification, investment committee simulation |
| [alirezarezvani/claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills) | MIT | A virtual executive team with a chief-of-staff router and artifact handoffs between roles |
| [emotixco/claude-skills-founder](https://github.com/emotixco/claude-skills-founder) | MIT | A multi-dimension idea scorecard ending in a build / pivot / kill verdict, MVP scoping, interview scripts |
| [wshobson/agents](https://github.com/wshobson/agents) (startup-business-analyst) | MIT | Bottom-up market sizing with triangulation, startup metrics by business model, team composition analysis |
| [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) (small-business, marketing, legal) | Apache-2.0 | Approval before anything touches money or customers, working from pasted data when nothing is connected, severity-by-likelihood legal risk |
| [claude-office-skills/skills](https://github.com/claude-office-skills/skills) | MIT | The starter document kit (invoice, proposal, NDA and contract outlines, expense tracking, weekly report) and brand guidelines |

Frameworks referenced by name belong to their authors: Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya), *The Mom Test* (Rob Fitzpatrick), *Obviously Awesome* positioning (April Dunford), *Traction* and the Bullseye framework (Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares), Jobs to be Done (Clayton Christensen and others), the Van Westendorp price sensitivity meter, and AARRR (Dave McClure).
